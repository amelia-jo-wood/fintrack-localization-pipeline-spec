# Getting Started with FinTrack

Welcome to the **FinTrack** family! Managing your cash flow across the Americas has never been simpler. Follow these three steps to finalize your system initialization:

## 1. Synchronize Your Financial Institutions
Click the **Link Account** button on your primary dashboard screen. We utilize a secure bank integration protocol powered by Plaid to encrypt your incoming data stream.

> *Note:* FinTrack will never store your direct banking credentials or PINs.

## 2. Configure Your Monthly Budget Parameters
Navigate to `Settings > Budgets` to define your strict spending caps. If your real-time tracking indicates that you have exceeded your monthly budget limit, an automated push alert will route to your device immediately.
